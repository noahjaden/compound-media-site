About me

I make it
and I run it.

No account manager in between, so when you text me, you get me.

Two years ago I made my first commercial in my bedroom in Groningen, with tools that had just come out, and it looked terrible. Around the twentieth it suddenly looked like film.

That's when it clicked: an ad that looks like film doesn't need a photographer or a shoot day anymore, just a hook in the first second, a story your customer recognises and an offer that adds up. I do the production myself, in days.

That's how Compound Media started, as one person with no account manager passing your work to a junior. I write your ads, make them and put them live in your own account, and every week you get a report you can read without a dictionary.

Why Compound?

Compound means it stacks, because every good ad and every good month builds on the last. One hit is nice, but a system that gets better every week is what makes you money.

What I stand for

A good ad doesn't have to cost a fortune. Thirty seconds of film without a big brand's production budget is possible now, so I do it.

Speed wins. More variants a month means you learn faster what works, so what brings in customers gets more money and the rest goes off.

You text me, you get me. WhatsApp, call or email and you have an answer within one working day, not three days later.

Your revenue first and my invoice second. If nothing comes out of it you pay me nothing, so name one agency that dares to do that.
